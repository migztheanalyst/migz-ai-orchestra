## Maintainer expectations

Maintainers are expected to:

- review changes for scope, safety, reproducibility, and maintainability
- keep public claims about adoption and benchmarks evidence-based
- disclose conflicts of interest when relevant
- avoid merging security-sensitive changes without adequate verification
- preserve a contributor-friendly path for new backends, docs, tests, and portability work
- keep the public core usable without private credentials

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer status is earned through sustained, high-quality contributions and reliable review behavior. There is no fixed contribution count.

Signals include:

- repeated accepted pull requests
- useful issue triage and review
- ownership of an integration or subsystem
- security-conscious engineering
- dependable participation in releases

Governance details are in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
