# Code of Conduct

MIGZ AI ORCHESTRA is built for an international community. Participation requires respectful, professional collaboration.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being constructive and specific in technical feedback
- welcoming contributors with different experience levels
- respecting different viewpoints, cultures, and backgrounds
- focusing disagreement on ideas, evidence, and code
- taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks, spam, or deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community or project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
